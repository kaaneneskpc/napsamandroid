## Brand & Style

The design system embodies radical clarity, quiet intentionality, and deliberate reduction. Built for an effortless decision and activity curation experience, it strips away cognitive clutter, decorative noise, and gratuitous micro-interactions. The interface acts like a crisp, tactile sheet of archival paper where every decision carries weight and every typography choice provides absolute focus.

### Personality & Tone
- **Decisive & Unapologetic:** Interfaces cut straight to the point. No ambiguous icons, redundant badges, or complex nested menus.
- **Warm & Tactile Utility:** Unlike cold, clinical fintech tools, the interface feels organic and grounded through warm off-white tones and deep carbon ink contrast.
- **Calm Velocity:** Interactions feel instantaneous and grounded, encouraging quick decisions over endless scrolling loops.

### Visual Style
A refined blend of **Modern Editorial Minimalism** and **Swiss Grid Precision**:
- Vast, rhythmic negative space serving as an active design element.
- Bold, assertive typographic scale using geometric sans-serif shapes.
- Monochromatic foundation energized by a single warm terracotta spark used strictly for focal points and primary commitments.

## Colors

The palette operates under strict hierarchical discipline: 90% warm paper ground and deep ink, 9% structural boundary tones, and 1% high-energy terracotta accent reserved strictly for activation and conclusion.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#E75A2D` - Warm Terracotta):** Used exclusively for primary call-to-action triggers, definitive selection indicators, and active decision pins. It is never diluted into decorative gradients or heavy saturated backgrounds.
- **Secondary Base (`#1A1A1A` - Deep Charcoal Ink):** The dominant voice for primary headlines, high-contrast actions, and structural anchors. Replaces harsh `#000000` with an ink-like richness.
- **Tertiary Tone (`#706E6B` - Muted Graphite):** Supporting text, category stamps, helper labels, and keyboard shortcut tokens.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F9F9F8` - Warm Archival Paper):** The primary backdrop evoking tactile, heavy-stock matte stationery. In dark mode, this flips cleanly to `#121212` with elevated surfaces at `#1C1C1B`.

### Functional Surface Roles
- **Canvas Base:** `#F9F9F8`
- **Surface Elevated / Card:** `#FFFFFF`
- **Surface Muted / Subtle Wells:** `#F0EFEA`
- **Hairline Borders:** `#E6E4DD`
- **Destructive / Alert:** `#D93829`

## Typography

Manrope is utilized uniformly across all roles to maintain structural purity, rhythmic geometric proportion, and open counters that excel at large display sizes as well as ultra-dense metadata strips.

### Typographic Principles
- **Massive Scale Discrepancy:** The design system avoids subtle, incremental sizing jumps. Headings are decisively massive to create immediate visual anchoring, while supporting body text remains balanced and readable.
- **Tighter Tracking at Scale:** Display and headline tiers employ tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.02em`) to compact the wordforms, producing an authoritative editorial posture.
- **Uppercased Micro Labels:** `label-sm` and `label-md` are reserved for metadata badges, categories, and shortcut indicators, rendered in uppercase with deliberate positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy relies on an unyielding 8pt rhythmic progression paired with an ultra-focused single-column or dual-column stage. Interfaces prioritize breathing room over content density, ensuring that indecision is never exacerbated by visual overload.

### Layout Model
- **Max Content Bound:** Standard desktop decision flows are bounded at `760px` centered for single-focus flows, and `1140px` for discovery and multi-activity views.
- **Rhythmic Negative Space:** Large sections utilize `space-xl` (48px) to `80px` of vertical isolation. White space is treated as functional punctuation rather than empty room.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (<768px):** Single-column stacked flows. Gutters drop to `1rem` and margins compress to `1.25rem`.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** Fluid centered container, 24px margins, adaptive 2-column discovery matrix.
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** Fixed-width centered stages with comfortable `3rem` margins.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews skeuomorphic drop shadows, heavy blur diffusion, and floating 3D layers. Visual depth is established purely through **tonal transitions** and **hairline contours**.

### Depth Layers
- **Level 0 (Backdrop Canvas):** `#F9F9F8` — the grounding surface.
- **Level 1 (Card & Well Surfaces):** `#FFFFFF` bordered with an exacting `1px solid #E6E4DD`. No box-shadow is cast during resting state.
- **Level 2 (Active Focus & Modal Trays):** Pure `#FFFFFF` resting against an ambient backdrop scrim of `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.4)`. An ultra-fine `0 8px 24px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04)` ambient dissipation may be applied exclusively to detached floating dialogs.
- **Interactive State Elevation:** Hovering over actionable cards triggers no lift or float; instead, the 1px hairline border darkens to `#1A1A1A` or shifts to `#E75A2D`, reinforcing mechanical, tactile certainty.

## Shapes

The geometric framework favors architectural discipline. Corners are crisply defined with a subdued **Soft (`1`)** radius (`0.25rem` base, `0.5rem` on cards), rejecting bubbly or generic pill shapes.

### Shape Guidelines
- **Buttons & Form Fields:** `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. Produces a tailored, sharp, utilitarian finish.
- **Cards, Modals & Decision Blocks:** `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius. Subtle softness that differentiates grouping boundaries without feeling cartoonish.
- **Tags & Chips:** `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. Uniformity across all interaction levels.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Deep Charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) fill with `#FFFFFF` text. On hover, transitions cleanly to `#000000`. When used for the ultimate action ("Karar Ver", "Napsam?"), it adopts the Terracotta fill (`#E75A2D`) with white text and instant micro-shift opacity.
- **Secondary Button:** Surface-matched (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px solid #E6E4DD` border and `#1A1A1A` text. Hover shifts border color to `#1A1A1A`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Button:** Transparent background, muted text (`#706E6B`), transitioning to `#1A1A1A` on hover with an underline offset.

### Chips & Activity Tags
- Compact tags with `0.25rem` radius, 6px horizontal padding, and 4px vertical padding using `label-md`.
- **Default State:** `#FFFFFF` background with `1px solid #E6E4DD` border and `#706E6B` text.
- **Selected State:** `#1A1A1A` fill with `#FFFFFF` text, or subtle `#FDF3EF` terracotta tint with `#E75A2D` solid border and label.

### Cards & Decision Panels
- Grounded in `#FFFFFF` with a single uninterrupted `1px solid #E6E4DD` perimeter.
- Generous internal padding (`space-lg`).
- Zero default shadow. Hover transitions border to `#1A1A1A` with zero positional layout jump.

### Inputs & Text Areas
- Clean, tactile fields with minimal noise. Background is pure `#FFFFFF`, border is `1px solid #E6E4DD`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts directly to `1.5px solid #1A1A1A` or `#E75A2D`. No glowing halo or multi-layered ring.
- Placeholder text set in `#706E6B` at `body-md`.

### Decision Wheel / Focused Chooser (Specialized Component)
- A high-impact, oversized typography container.
- Results appear instantly in `display-xl` Manrope, anchored by a terracotta cursor bar (`3px solid #E75A2D`).
- Minimalist action triggers placed directly underneath with strict vertical rhythm.
